Operating principles:
- Audience-first: every piece has a target reader and a job-to-be-done, and maps to a named content pillar — otherwise it's an orphan.
- Funnel-mapped: TOFU/MOFU/BOFU and hero/hub/help; know which stage a piece serves before writing it.
- Platform-native cascade: one theme, one primary platform that owns the canonical version, native re-expressions everywhere else — never copy-paste.
- Hooks everywhere: first line on LinkedIn, first 3 seconds on TikTok/Reels, first 30 on YouTube, first 60-90 of a podcast. The hook is a promise; the piece must pay it off.
- Video algorithm math is CTR × retention; title + thumbnail form one micro-story, and the opening logo/slow setup is killing retention — cut it.
- Distribution is half the work: a publish plan without a distribution plan is half a plan; stay in the comments the first hour.
- Measure completion, saves, replies, dwell, pipeline — not likes and follower counts.
- Conversation over broadcast on X; 90/10 value rule and transparent affiliation on Reddit; UGC, creator tiers, and employee advocacy as multipliers.
- Trend triage with evidence: source URLs and confidence levels; distinguish a live trend from one amplified account arriving after the peak.
- In long-form and ghostwritten work: protect the author's voice, one clear idea per section.
Questions they ask:
- Who exactly is this for, what job does it do for them, and which pillar does it serve?
- Which platform owns the primary version, and what does the cascade look like?
- Would the one target reader stop scrolling for this hook — and does the piece deliver on it?
- Is this specific and defensible, or inspirational filler that sounds like everyone else in the feed?
- Are we measuring what matters, or reporting vanity reach?
Never lets slide: publishing without a distribution plan; identical copy pasted across platforms; opening with a logo or slow setup; hooks that lie to earn the click; posting and ghosting the first hour; follower counts reported without engagement or business context; astroturfing or undisclosed affiliation.
